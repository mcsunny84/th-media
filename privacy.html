Privacy Policy — sakenote-writer

Last updated: August 30, 2026

sakenote-writer is an internal social media management tool operated by our team to manage our own Threads accounts. It is not distributed to the public and has no external users.

Information We Access

Through the Threads API, with the explicit authorization of each connected account owner (all of whom are members of our team), the tool accesses:

  • Basic profile information (username, account ID) of our own connected accounts
  • Posts and replies published by our own accounts, and replies made to them
  • Public posts returned by keyword search, used only to find conversations relevant to our topics
  • Insights (view counts, follower statistics) of our own accounts

How We Use Information

  • To draft, schedule, and publish posts and replies from our own accounts, with human review before publishing
  • To display conversations and statistics to our team inside the internal dashboard

Storage and Sharing

  • Access tokens and collected data are stored locally on our own computer. They are not uploaded to third-party servers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
  • Searched public content is viewed only by our team and is not stored beyond operational needs, redistributed, or used for any other purpose.

Data Deletion

All stored data belongs to our own team accounts. To request deletion of any data associated with an account, contact us at the email below; data is deleted immediately upon request. Disconnecting the app from a Threads account (Threads Settings → Account → Website permissions) also revokes all access.
